ABOUT

Research with industrial impact.

My work combines modern AI with physics-based modeling to solve hard inverse problems in geoscience. I focus on generative modeling, reinforcement learning, multimodal AI, and agentic workflows that can reason over scientific tools, simulations, and domain knowledge.

I have worked across energy technology and applied research, developing methods for seismic imaging, full-waveform inversion, low-frequency extrapolation, scientific foundation models, and AI-assisted subsurface interpretation.
